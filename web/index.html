Keen 5 and 6 are commercial: bring your own copy. Pick the game folder, or the files EGAGRAPH.CKn GAMEMAPS.CKn AUDIO.CKn, or a .zip containing them (you can also drop them here). They stay in this browser (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded.

Click a button to start (this also enables audio). Saves and options persist in this browser.

Controls: arrows move, Ctrl jump, Alt pogo (with Modern pogo: hold Alt for pogo + jump, let go to release both; a quick tap keeps the pogo on), Space fire, Enter status, Esc menu, F11 or the Fullscreen button for full screen. With X-ray ticked, F10+H turns it off and on in game.
Ctrl+W/T/N and Alt+Tab/Alt+F4 are reserved by the browser or OS and cannot be used in-game.
